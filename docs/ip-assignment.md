# IP Assignment — TollChat

**Repository status:** record copy; the signed original retained outside this
repository is authoritative.

**Effective date:** 2026-07-27

**Assignor:** Ryan Prasad ("Assignor")

**Assignee:** Benevolent Clankers LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("Assignee")

## Recital

Assignor created the tollchat.ai website, its source code, content, and
the associated "TollChat" name and branding prior to the formation of
Assignee.

## Assignment

For good and valuable consideration, including Assignee's assumption of
ownership and operation of the work, Assignor hereby assigns to Assignee
all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to:

- the copyrights in the tollchat.ai website, its source code, and its content;
- the trademark and trade-name rights in "TollChat" and the tollchat.ai domain;

together with all associated goodwill, effective as of the date above.
