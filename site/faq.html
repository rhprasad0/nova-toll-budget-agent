How TollChat prices a trip.

TollChat reports source-backed toll records. These are the short answers on where the numbers come from, when they were observed, and where the available data stops.

01 · Sources

Where do TollChat prices come from?

Dynamic prices for I-66 Inside the Beltway, the 95/395 Express Lanes, and the 495 Express Lanes come from VDOT’s public toll-pricing feeds. Dulles Toll Road and Dulles Greenway use committed, hand-transcribed 2-axle E-ZPass rate tables reconstructed from operator pages and cross-checked against other public sources. They exclude pay-by-plate and 3+ axle rates; the Exit 16 directional rule and weekday-only peak windows are documented assumptions. TollChat is independent and is not affiliated with VDOT or any toll operator.

02 · Freshness

Why can TollChat differ from an operator site?

For 95/395/495, VDOT republishes the same Transurban price series 10 minutes later: 59,217 aligned comparisons matched exactly. That validates transport and time alignment, not independent toll-price accuracy. I-66 publishes in six-minute intervals with a measured 1–4-minute source delay, so TollChat may retain the preceding VDOT capture until the next poll. Every dynamic result includes the VDOT observation time; use the operator links above for the price displayed now.

03 · Route oracle

How does the route oracle work?

The route oracle is a committed, read-only route map derived from operator-published entry and exit pairs, public maps and explicitly curated connector facts. It stores route topology and price lookup keys; it does not generate prices. The planner selects supported legs, corridor tools retrieve the relevant VDOT observation or committed Dulles rate table, and TollChat lists and adds the returned toll components. If the oracle cannot support a route, TollChat does not invent one.

04 · Known gap

Why is part of the I-95 ↔ I-495 junction unpriced?

The available VDOT data does not provide a price for the road between the direction-selected I-95 boundary—Edsall or Franconia-Springfield—and I-495 Near Braddock Road. We made a deliberate design decision to leave that gap unpriced instead of calling it free or fabricating a fare. TollChat reports the priced components and labels their sum the known toll total. It is not a complete operator-issued fare.

05 · Hallucinations

Does TollChat hallucinate?

Yes, it can. TollChat uses GPT-5.6 Luna. In 3,400 frozen, tool-disabled responses, our deterministic screen found no dollar amount absent from the supplied toll evidence. That is a narrow result, not a claim that every answer was correct.

The model invented one observation time. In the multi-leg tests, 61 responses omitted required components, totals, or partial-price disclosures; those categories overlap. Another 540 responses could not be automatically verified because equal-valued toll components make token matching insufficient to prove correct attribution.

More hallucination evaluations are planned across broader scenarios. The current battery does not test live route selection, tool invocation, source accuracy or freshness, production traffic, or abuse resistance.
