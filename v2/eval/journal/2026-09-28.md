## October 3: controlled inference cost benchmark

The cost dashboard's inference section measured 12 existing development cases
three times with frozen tool results: corpus 3.3.28, harness 2.3.28, and
cost-benchmark runner 1.0.0. Real Luna 6 calls used actor low, TollChat medium, and
judges xhigh, with existing prompts and output limits. These settings applied
only to this cost run; runtime/eval defaults and calibration gates stayed unchanged.

All 36 conversations had complete usage accounting and produced 47 completed
assistant turns. Three stopped on tool-argument violations; their charges remain
included. There were no unknown-usage calls or inconclusive conversations.
Standard token rates verified October 3 produced these costs:

| Role | Calls | Total inference cost | Average per attempted conversation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| TollChat agent | 85 | $0.052428 | $0.001456 |
| Simulated user | 47 | $0.007546 | $0.000210 |
| Evaluation judges | 108 | $0.089478 | $0.002485 |
| Combined | 240 | **$0.149452** | **$0.004151** |

Agent charges divided by completed turns were **$0.001115 per answer**, or
**$1.115488 per 1,000 answers** at the measured average. Total spending stayed
below the $2 ceiling; an independent per-call calculation reconciled exactly
with the public aggregate. About 98% of agent input and 89% of judge input were
cache reads. This sample does not establish production traffic mix, cold-cache
cost, evaluation reliability, or public-chat attribution. Infrastructure and
tool execution are excluded.
