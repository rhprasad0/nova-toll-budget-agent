### I-95/I-495 identity-proxy validation

For 16 missing OD IDs, `identity_proxy_v1` provisionally copies a mapped VDOT
price. A chronological 70/30 split of July 25-30 captures yielded 1,200 holdout
comparisons: **$0.106 MAE, 96.1% within $0.50, $0.00 p95 absolute error, and
$8.05 maximum error**. Analysis cost was unrecorded. Related feeds, clustered
observations, and the short overlap leave per-OD and seasonal accuracy unproven.
Keep modeled labels and withhold an estimate without an eligible row. Stronger
claims need per-OD/direction results and reproducible pairing evidence; the
[mapping guidance](../results/i95-missing-od-pricing.md) retains those limits.
