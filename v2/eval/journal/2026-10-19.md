## October 24: upgrade certifi and aiohttp, refreeze corpus 5.2.19

certifi 2026.7.22 still trusted emSign root certificates whose intermediate keys
were compromised (advisory GHSA-6q48-6c7w-9gw2, critical). Trivy flagged it in
`uv.lock`, and because development delivery admits a release only when that scan
passes, every merge to main stopped deploying until it was fixed. certifi moved to
**2026.10.19**, the first fixed release. The pending Dependabot upgrade of aiohttp
to **3.14.4** (with yarl 1.25.1) came along, so the corpus refreezes once. Both
reach the deployed agent and timed-check packages, which were rebuilt; the loader,
publisher and chat-proxy packages are unchanged.

`uv.lock` is a protected eval source, so all three splits were refrozen as corpus
**5.2.19**. Cases, prompt points and fixtures are byte-identical to 5.2.18. Claude
recorded input approval at Ryan's request, and no one inspected the cases.

the harness 2.5.19 calibration still predates the October 16 and 24 dependency

combined calibration. No baseline was run.

Decision: **merge the upgrade and keep deferring the calibration.**
