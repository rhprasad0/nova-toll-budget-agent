Methods · Limits · Privacy

How TollChat estimates a commute.

The useful short version: TollChat constrains the model to reviewed route and pricing tools, shows where evidence stops, and publishes the code and evaluation method so you can check the homework.

01 · Sources

Where do the toll prices come from?

Dynamic I-66 Inside the Beltway and 95/395/495 Express Lanes evidence comes from VDOT’s public pricing feeds. The Dulles Toll Road and Dulles Greenway use reviewed 2-axle E-ZPass schedules reconstructed from operator-published rates. TollChat is independent and is not affiliated with VDOT or a toll operator.

02 · Two questions

How are current prices different from annual ballparks?

A current-price request uses the latest supported observation or published fixed rate for one route. An annual ballpark uses recent complete same-date round trips to produce daily P25, P50, and P90 scenarios, then multiplies those daily values by the chosen commute days. Those are historical scenarios—not predictions of next year’s tolls.

03 · Map

What do the four map pins mean?

Each pin’s primary number is the P50 annual toll for a 2-axle E-ZPass commute to Washington, Monday through Friday, leaving at 8:30 AM and returning at 5:30 PM for 240 commute days. The detail panel shows P25 and P90, the evidence coverage, and the snapshot time. The figures exclude untolled road costs and are neither forecasts nor operator quotes.

04 · Route oracle

How does TollChat keep the model on supported roads?

The model resolves ordinary place names to bounded route points. A read-only oracle validates the directed route and returns fixed pricing keys; the pricing tools—not the model—retrieve and add supported toll components. An unsupported route becomes an explicit unavailable result rather than an improvised fare.

05 · Known gaps

Can a supported trip still be incomplete?

Yes. TollChat discloses missing observations, partial historical coverage, modeled data, and unpriced general-purpose connections. In particular, published data does not expose every I-95 ↔ I-495 origin-destination price. Known components may still be useful, but an omitted gap is never silently called free.

06 · Hallucinations

Does TollChat hallucinate?

Yes, it can. TollChat uses GPT-5.6 Luna. In one frozen Springfield-Franconia–Westpark annual-ballpark evaluation, 996 of 1,000 responses (99.6%) made no quantitative claim outside the supplied tool evidence under the strict policy.

One response changed the supplied 5:31 PM time to 1:31 PM. Three responses performed correct scenario-difference arithmetic that was absent from the payload, which violated the strict evidence policy without producing incorrect arithmetic. Core semantic compliance was 97.1%; adding the explicit TollChat-attribution requirement and strict quantitative policy produced the conservative 93.1% end-to-end result.

This repeated-generation test measures reliability for one production-shaped fixture. It is not an agent-wide hallucination rate, route-coverage result, source-accuracy test, or production-traffic evaluation. Read the complete report and methodology.

07 · Privacy and support

What does this preview collect?

The browser includes no analytics or behavioral-tracking code. The loopback server keeps conversations in memory until reset or restart and does not write them to disk. OpenAI processes requests. A random per-tab session ID keeps follow-ups together and may appear in a server error log, but TollChat cannot see a browser-side failure or reconstruct one from client telemetry.

The project is open source under Apache-2.0. Please report bugs or privacy questions to contact@tollchat.ai.
