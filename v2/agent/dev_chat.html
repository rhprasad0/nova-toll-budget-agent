Sources, privacy & estimate limits

Ask what a supported trip costs now or how a tolled commute could affect your annual take-home pay. TollChat uses reasonable tax and vehicle-cost assumptions to give you a rough take-home-pay estimate.*

We observed no fabricated costs in our 1,000-answer test. Toll and take-home-pay figures come from code, not AI arithmetic. 996 responses (99.6%) used only supplied numbers. One misstated a time. See the test limits.

New public usage counting and daily publication have stopped. Historical aggregate and snapshot data are retained and are not being purged in this release. We make an effort to protect your personal information by automatically redacting detected PII from diagnostic traces before storage. Automated redaction can miss information. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal details. Diagnostic traces may include redacted conversations and tool results; trace archives expire after seven days. It sends prompts, prior conversation context, and responses to OpenAI with Responses storage disabled. OpenAI still keeps abuse-monitoring logs, which may include that content, for up to 30 days by default. We accepted that tradeoff to keep TollChat free to use. Read how OpenAI handles API data and our privacy notice.

The code is open source. TollChat cannot see client-side failures, so send bug reports to contact@tollchat.ai.

TollChat is independent and is not affiliated with VDOT or any toll operator. It uses public data from VDOT SmarterRoads and rates published by toll operators. Source data may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. Verify current rates and road conditions with the relevant operator before travel. TollChat is not a navigation or emergency service. Its answers are estimates, not forecasts, tax calculations, financial plans, or quotes from toll operators. Use them for general information only. Do not rely on them for financial or employment decisions, tax filing, legal matters, or other important decisions.
